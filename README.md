# oneliner-laughs

## Entry

One original one-liner about a smart fridge that gets a little too honest at 3 a.m. — it starts like a story about a gadget, then the punchline turns the joke back on the person telling it.

`main.ts` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My smart fridge texted me at 3 a.m.: the leftover pizza and I have a lot in common — we're both cold, unwanted, and past our best-by date.

## How to run

    node main.ts

The entry lands in `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set, otherwise in `/output/entry.txt`.

## Note

The fridge has since been unplugged, and the narrator has since ordered takeaway. The pizza remains, unbothered and unclaimed.
